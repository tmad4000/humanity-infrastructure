The Premise

Imagine it were everyone's birthright to access the sum-total of human know-how.

The Codex is a hub of knowledge resources and open-source infrastructure — built to bring human flourishing into every person's reach. We envision something most of the developed world eventually becomes part of, to a greater or lesser degree.

📖

Knowledge as a public good

Survival and thriving guides, medical know-how, open curricula, role-model casting, the craftsmanship that is all software. Shouldn't software from 10 years ago be free by now?

🛠️

Tools that make people independent

Open reputation systems, collective-action tools, personal-leverage automation, assistive tech. Hacked-together infrastructure that helps people help themselves.

🤝

Coordinate as a superorganism

Guilds taking on epic quests. Global sense-making. Volunteerism, philanthropy for everyone, and ways to pitch in on the problems that matter — together.

🌱

Flourishing in reach for all

From "how to thrive as a highly sensitive person on a budget" to "how to rebuild society from scratch." Compassionate, practical, and free.

Lastbook

lastbook.org

Need Knowledge that survives when the internet doesn't

A free, open-source offline library of practical manuals, videos and maps, with optional local AI. Knowledge for preparedness and rebuilding, available after setup without an internet connection.

  • Open source · MIT
  • Mac · Windows · Linux
  • By farbood & contributors

Editor's Note: when the network goes down, the knowledge shouldn't. A library you can hand to a neighbour on a drive.

SignuDoc

github.com/NYLLON-SOFTWARE/SignuDoc

Need Open document signing infrastructure

Open-source e-signatures. Send, sign and seal documents from your own cloud at $0 per seat, with audit trails and tamper-evident seals.

  • Open source · MIT
  • Self-hosted on your Vercel account

Editor's Note: agreeing on things is basic civic plumbing. Signing a PDF shouldn't need a per-seat licence or someone else's cloud.

Paxi

paxi.fyi

Need Use the best service for each task, without interface lock-in

Compares live ride prices and times across Uber, Lyft, Waymo, Tesla, Zoox, Curb and more, then books the one you choose. Your logins stay on your phone.

  • Free · closed source
  • iPhone (iOS 17+)
  • By Doug Feigelson, a friend of the maintainer

Editor's Note: every app wants to be the only door. You should be able to compare providers, choose the best one for each job and switch freely. Community submission #2.
