About the project

Farbood and the Lastbook contributors bring resources for everyday needs and emergencies into one downloadable library. Humanity Infrastructure features their work in its public directory.

Browse online

Ideaflow hosts a public copy you can open in your browser. It needs an internet connection and opens without an account. How the hosted edition works.

Use it offline

Get the original app and download a library pack while you have a connection. You can then browse that pack on your own computer. The original project also offers optional local AI.
