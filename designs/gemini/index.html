Need 01

Knowledge that survives when the internet doesn't

Practical manuals, maps and how-to videos that keep working offline, so people can care for each other through outages and rebuild after them.

Lastbook
MIT (open source) Mac, Windows, Linux

A free, open-source offline library of practical manuals, videos and maps, with optional local AI.

Maker: farbood and contributors Code: GitHub
Draft Editor's Note "When the network goes down, the knowledge shouldn't. Lastbook is a library you can hand to a neighbour on a drive."

Need 02

Open document signing infrastructure

Agreeing on things is basic civic plumbing. Signing a PDF shouldn't require a per-seat licence or handing your agreements to someone else's cloud.

SignuDoc
MIT (open source) Vercel self-host

Open-source e-signatures: send, sign and seal documents from your own cloud, $0 per seat, with audit trails and tamper-evident seals.

Maker: Kevin Rose / NYLLON Code: NYLLON-SOFTWARE/SignuDoc
Draft Editor's Note "A small team was quoted $8,100 a year to sign PDFs. Infrastructure this basic should belong to the people using it."
See more in the Directory of the Internet →
Need 03

Use the best service for each task, without interface lock-in

Every app wants to be the only door. People should be able to compare and choose the best provider for each job, and switch freely.

Paxi
Closed source Maker is a friend of the maintainer iPhone (iOS 17+)

Compares live ride prices and times across Uber, Lyft, Waymo, Tesla, Zoox, Curb and more, then books the one you choose. Logins stay on your phone.

Maker: Doug Feigelson (Passenger Labs) Policy Disclosure: Free to use, proprietary binary. Included as an exemplar of aggregate interfaces.
Draft Editor's Note "One fare board instead of five apps. This is the pattern I want everywhere: you choose the service, the interface works for you."
