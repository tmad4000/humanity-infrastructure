Shelves

About the Directory of the Internet
  1. Need 01

    Knowledge that survives when the internet doesn't

    Practical manuals, maps and how-to videos that keep working offline, so people can care for each other through outages and rebuild after them.

  2. Need 02

    Open document signing infrastructure

    Agreeing on things is basic civic plumbing. Signing a PDF shouldn't require a per-seat licence or handing your agreements to someone else's cloud.

    See more in the Directory of the Internet
    Featured

    SignuDoc

    MIT · open source Self-hosted

    Open-source e-signatures: send, sign and seal documents from your own cloud, $0 per seat, with audit trails and tamper-evident seals.

    License
    MIT (open source)
    Platforms
    Self-hosted on your own Vercel account
    Maker
    Kevin Rose / NYLLON
    Source
    Editor's note Draft

    “A small team was quoted $8,100 a year to sign PDFs. Infrastructure this basic should belong to the people using it.”

    Draft editor's note: a proposal awaiting the maintainer's own words.
    See more in the Directory of the Internet
  3. Need 03

    Use the best service for each task, without interface lock-in

    Every app wants to be the only door. People should be able to compare and choose the best provider for each job, and switch freely.

    See more in the Directory of the Internet
    Featured

    Paxi

    Closed source Free Maker is a friend of the maintainer

    Compares live ride prices and times across Uber, Lyft, Waymo, Tesla, Zoox, Curb and more, then books the one you choose. Logins stay on your phone.

    License
    Free; closed source
    Platforms
    iPhone (iOS 17+)
    Maker
    Doug Feigelson (Passenger Labs) — a friend of the maintainer
    paxi.fyi Submission (issue #2)
    Editor's note Draft

    “One fare board instead of five apps. This is the pattern I want everywhere: you choose the service, the interface works for you.”

    Draft editor's note: a proposal awaiting the maintainer's own words.

Participate

Six ways in, from a two-minute suggestion to building the next shelf

Start wherever fits. Every rung is useful on its own.

  1. Suggest a resource

    A short form, no coding. Tell us what belongs on a shelf and why.

    Open the form

  2. Open a pull request

    Add or correct a listing in the repository. The contribution guide explains the format and the inclusion policy.

    Read the contribution guide

  3. Claim a quest on the Build List

    Pick something that is missing and put your name next to it.

    Go to the board

  4. Join the World Quest Guild

    Work on quests alongside other people.

    quests.world

  5. Join Humanity Infrastructure Builders Coming soon

    A planned group on Unlinked for people building and maintaining the things on these shelves.

  6. Get the occasional letter Planned

    Low volume. New shelves, new quests, and who built what.
